## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, high-craft digital canvas for an independent studio practice operating at the intersection of architectural discipline and contemporary digital art direction. The identity rejects conventional agency maximalism in favor of dark luxury minimalism, Swiss modernist structural hierarchy, and surgical typography.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Singular Authorship:** Uncompromising attention to micro-details, deliberate pacing, and bespoke craftsmanship that signals individual mastery over collective dilution.
- **Editorial Gravity:** Monumental typographic scale paired with hairline rules, evoking archival monographs, high-end architectural publications, and bespoke independent press.
- **Tactile Restraint:** Quiet deep surfaces punctuated by intentional phosphor-like electric micro-accents. Every graphic gesture is functional, deliberate, and sparse.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Global founders, design-led tech executives, luxury ateliers, and cultural institutions seeking uncompromising, bespoke digital presence.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, high technical authority, exclusivity, and understated Mediterranean sophistication filtered through rigorous European graphic discipline.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated low-luminance deep tones, anchored by near-black surfaces and warm off-white typography, preserving eyeshot comfort while elevating high-contrast readability. Accent coloration is strictly surgical—reserved for availability state signals, interactive focus cues, and system feedback.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0B0C`):** The foundational absolute background. Deep charcoal-black with subtle warm undertones to eliminate screen harshness.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#121214`):** Base container, card substrate, and structural panel background.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1A1A1E`):** Hover states, elevated drawers, input fields, and pill backgrounds.
- **Text Primary (`#F2F1EC`):** Warm chalk off-white; high contrast against dark surfaces without the clinical glare of `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Secondary (`#8E8D8A`):** Archival stone tone for editorial metadata, secondary narratives, and subheadings.
- **Text Subdued (`#555558`):** Low-hierarchy annotations, timestamps, inactive states, and footnotes.
- **Accent High-Voltage Green (`#00E37A`):** Restrained, vivid phosphor green. Used exclusively for live availability indicators, selected filter dots, and precise micro-accents.
- **Structural Hairlines (`#222226` / `#2A2A30`):** 1px physical dividing lines delineating layout blocks, modular grids, and interactive containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates tension between expressive modernist display grotesks and strict technological monospaced metadata.

### Typographic Roles & Rules
- **Space Grotesk:** Drives high-impact hero claims, case-study headlines, and editorial statements. Rendered with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.04em`) to simulate hot-metal press density.
- **Inter:** Handles running project prose, philosophical statements, and narrative case study breakdowns. Provides neutral legibility and uncolored functional clarity.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Manages index codes, project dates, client sectors, deliverables lists, and metric indicators. Always set with open tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) and uppercase styling where classifying tags or status flags are rendered.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an asymmetric Swiss modular grid system that combines expansive horizontal breathing space with strictly delineated 1px hairline rules.

### Grid & Responsive Mechanics
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `4rem` outer canvas padding and `2.5rem` gutters. Section modules alternate between 8/4, 7/5, and full-span arrangements to create rhythm without chaotic asymmetry.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margin and `1.5rem` gutters. Case-study metadata shifts from side rails into stacked horizontal bars above the imagery.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas margin. Elements stack strictly vertical with section demarcations relying on top hairline borders rather than artificial floating boxes.

### Spatial Discipline
- Vertical pacing between primary editorial sections relies on `space-3xl` (`7rem`) to enforce calm and spatial permanence.
- Internal component groupings use `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) to maintain structural tightness within bounding hairlines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows and synthetic elevation blurs in favor of architectural surface sequencing and planar separation.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#0B0B0C`):** Base structural void.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#121214`):** Solid case-study modules, browser presentation frames, and drawer panels. Outlined with a continuous `1px` border of `#222226`.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1A1A1E`):** Floating controls, active filters, dropdown sheets, and active form fields. Outlined with `1px` `#2A2A30`.

### Depth Techniques
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Visual distinction is driven by `1px solid #222226` dividers. Hovering elevated blocks triggers a gentle border transition to `#2A2A30` and an optional interior surface shift to `#1A1A1E`.
- **Backdrop Diffusion:** Slide-out drawers and sticky navigation bars use a subtle dark translucent backing (`rgba(11, 11, 12, 0.85)`) coupled with a heavy `16px` backdrop filter blur to maintain separation against under-scrolling text without high-contrast shadows.

## Shapes

The physical silhouette language is sharp and architectural, favoring clean edges and micro-fillets over generic rounded pill geometry.

### Radius Calibration
- **Standard Containers & Cards (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Subtle soft radius that prevents aggressive harsh corners while retaining a precision-milled appearance.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Conforms strictly to the structural grid profile.
- **Status Pills & Micro Badges:** Full pill execution (`9999px`) allowed exclusively for metadata tags and status indicators to contrast against the rectangular structural geometry of the main grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid warm off-white surface (`#F2F1EC`), near-black typography (`#0B0B0C`), JetBrains Mono uppercase (`label-sm`), `4px` border radius, padded with `0.75rem` vertical and `1.5rem` horizontal. On hover: background shifts to `#00E37A` with instantaneous zero-friction color transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `1px solid #2A2A30`, text in `#F2F1EC`. Hover introduces `#121214` background and borders shift to `#F2F1EC`.
- **Editorial Link:** Text-only in `#F2F1EC` paired with an external angled arrow symbol `↗`. On hover, the arrow shifts `2px` top-right with color accenting in `#00E37A`.

### Status Indicators & Availability Pills
- **Availability Beacon:** Encapsulated pill container with background `#121214`, border `1px solid #222226`, and padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Features an inner `6px` radial dot of `#00E37A` paired with a continuous soft-fade pulse animation (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(0, 227, 122, 0.4)`), followed by `label-sm` text: `"AVAILABLE Q3 / Q4"`.

### Case-Study Cards
- **Structure:** Framed container (`#121214`) lined with `1px solid #222226`. Upper tier displays browser shell mockup with clean minimal chrome bar (`3` micro dots at `#2A2A30` and muted path URL in JetBrains Mono).
- **Metadata Header/Footer:** Two-tier modular data rail separated by hairline dividers. Columns show Client, Sector, Year, and Role in `code-num` (`#8E8D8A`), anchored by the main project title in `headline-md` (`#F2F1EC`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover produces a border accent transition to `#2A2A30` and subtle inner image scale (1.02x zoom over 400ms cubic bezier curve).

### Input Fields & Contact Drawer
- **Inputs & Textareas:** Dark surface (`#121214`), `1px solid #222226` hairline perimeter, `4px` radius. Typographic input in Inter `body-md` (`#F2F1EC`), label anchored above in JetBrains Mono `label-sm` (`#8E8D8A`). Active focus shifts border directly to `#00E37A` with no harsh glow ring.
- **Contact Drawer:** Right-aligned slide-out sheet (`480px` desktop width, 100vw mobile) draped over canvas with `#0B0B0C` background, left border `1px solid #222226`, and backdrop scrim darkened at 60% opacity.

### Filter Chips & Tags
- **Tag Style:** JetBrains Mono uppercase `11px`, letter-spacing `0.06em`. Encased in `#1A1A1E` with subtle border `#222226`. Selected active chip features leading dot `#00E37A` with white typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Architectural `16px x 16px` square, `2px` radius, border `1px solid #2A2A30`, background `#121214`. Checked state fills container with `#00E37A` and sharp dark check icon (`#0B0B0C`).
- **Radios:** `16px x 16px` circle, matching dark base, checked state renders an internal centered `6px` solid dot in `#00E37A`.
